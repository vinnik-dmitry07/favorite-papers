The core idea behind LSP is to address the inefficiencies of 'scattered acceptance,' where tokens are committed independently at various points in a sequence. This scattered approach, the authors argue, leads to fragmented Key-Value (KV) cache updates and increased computational overhead. LSP, in contrast, proposes a monolithic prefix absorption strategy, where the longest contiguous and stable prefix of the sequence is committed in a single step. The stability of a prefix is determined by a logit margin, which measures the difference between the top two logit values for each token.
