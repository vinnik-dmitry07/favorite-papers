The authors contrast two training approaches: standard fine-tuning (SFT) and implicit chain-of-thought (ICoT). They find that while SFT models fail to achieve high accuracy on 4x4 multiplication, ICoT models, which are trained with intermediate chain-of-thought tokens that are gradually removed, succeed.
