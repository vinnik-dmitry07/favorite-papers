The core challenge addressed by CDE is the well-documented issue of poor exploration in RLVR, which often leads to premature convergence and entropy collapse, hindering the model's ability to discover effective reasoning strategies. CDE tackles this by leveraging intrinsic curiosity signals from both the actor and critic components of the reinforcement learning framework. Specifically, the actor's curiosity is quantified using the perplexity of the generated response, while the critic's curiosity is measured by the variance in value estimates derived from a multi-head architecture.
