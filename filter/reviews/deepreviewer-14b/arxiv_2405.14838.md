This paper introduces a novel approach, Stepwise Internalization (ICoT-SI), aimed at enhancing the efficiency of chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning in large language models (LLMs). The core idea revolves around gradually removing intermediate reasoning steps during the fine-tuning process, effectively 'internalizing' the reasoning within the model's parameters. This method seeks to bridge the gap between the high accuracy of explicit CoT reasoning and the speed of direct, no-CoT approaches. The authors begin by training a model with explicit CoT, then progressively shorten the CoT during fine-tuning, using techniques like removal smoothing and optimizer resetting to stabilize the training process. Empirically, the study demonstrates that ICoT-SI achieves performance comparable to explicit CoT while significantly reducing inference time.
