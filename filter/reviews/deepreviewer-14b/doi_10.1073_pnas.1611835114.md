The core idea behind EWC is to selectively slow down learning on weights that are crucial for previously learned tasks. This is achieved by adding a penalty term to the loss function, which constrains important weights to stay close to their previously optimized values. The importance of each weight is determined by the Fisher Information Matrix (FIM), which is computed after learning each task.
