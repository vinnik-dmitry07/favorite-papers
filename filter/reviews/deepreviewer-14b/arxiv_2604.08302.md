## Summary:

This paper introduces DMax, a novel approach to enhance the parallel decoding capabilities of diffusion language models (dLLMs). The core contribution lies in addressing the error accumulation issue that plagues existing dLLMs when attempting aggressive parallel decoding. DMax achieves this through two primary innovations: On-Policy Uniform Training (OPUT) and Soft Parallel Decoding (SPD). OPUT is a training strategy that bridges the gap between training and inference by constructing noisy sequences on-policy from the model's own predictive distribution, rather than relying on a uniform distribution. This approach allows the model to learn to correct its own potential prediction errors. SPD, on the other hand, is an inference strategy that represents each intermediate decoding state as a hybrid soft embedding, formed by interpolating between the predicted token embedding and the mask embedding based on the model's prediction confidence.
