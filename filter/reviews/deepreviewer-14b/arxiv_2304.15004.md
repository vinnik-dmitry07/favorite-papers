The authors propose that what appears to be emergence is often an artifact of the evaluation metrics used. They argue that discontinuous or nonlinear metrics, such as accuracy on multiple-choice tasks or exact string match, can create the illusion of abrupt transitions, while a more continuous and linear metric, like token error rate, would reveal a smoother, more predictable improvement in model performance as model size increases.
