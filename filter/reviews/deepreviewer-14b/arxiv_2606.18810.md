Operating within the Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) framework, SC-GRPO addresses the limitations of existing methods that often rely on external rewards or process reward models. The core innovation of SC-GRPO lies in its self-conditioning mechanism, where the model uses its own verified trajectories to create a token-level credit assignment signal. This is achieved by calculating the Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence between the original model's token distributions and those conditioned on a verified solution. Instead of using this KL divergence as an additive loss term, SC-GRPO employs it as a multiplicative weight on the gradients within the GRPO framework.
