Unlike traditional on-policy distillation (OPD) methods, which suffer from high variance in gradient estimation and an information bottleneck due to the language modeling (LM) head, OPRD directly supervises the student model's intermediate hidden states using the teacher's, on the same on-policy rollouts. This approach provides deterministic, low-variance gradients and exposes richer structural information, leading to more efficient and effective distillation. The authors further extend OPRD to OPRD-Bridge, which enables cross-architecture distillation by leveraging a shared low-rank representation space between heterogeneous models.
