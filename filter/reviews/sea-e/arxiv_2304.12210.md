 **Summary:**
The paper provides a comprehensive review of self-supervised learning (SSL) methods, categorizing them into four main families: Deep Metric Learning, Self-Distillation, Canonical Correlation Analysis, and Masked Image Modeling. It discusses the theoretical and practical aspects of each family, including their historical development, methodologies, and empirical results. The paper also explores the role of data augmentation, projectors, and other components in SSL, offering insights into their impact on performance. Despite its comprehensive coverage, the paper is criticized for its lack of novelty and its reliance on existing literature without significant new contributions.
