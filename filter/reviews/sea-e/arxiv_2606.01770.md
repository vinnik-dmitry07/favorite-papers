 **Summary:**
The paper introduces Adaptive Auto-Harness, a framework designed to enhance the performance of language model (LLM) agents in open-ended task streams by addressing the challenges of unbounded task arrival, task heterogeneity, and non-stationarity. The framework employs a multi-agent evolver system, a harness tree with solve-time routing, and human-in-the-loop steering hooks to adapt to the evolving nature of these tasks. The paper presents a theoretical analysis of the regret decomposition and empirical evaluations across various benchmarks, demonstrating the framework's effectiveness in improving performance over existing auto-harness baselines. The authors also discuss the limitations of the approach and provide a detailed analysis of the system's components.
