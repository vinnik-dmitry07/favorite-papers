 **Summary:**
The paper provides a comprehensive review of photonic matrix multiplication (MVM) methods, focusing on their applications in optical signal processing and artificial neural networks (ANNs). It discusses three primary methods: plane light conversion (PLC), Mach-Zehnder interferometer (MZI), and wavelength division multiplexing (WDM), comparing their advantages and limitations. The paper also explores the integration of photonic MVM into optical neural networks (ONNs) and discusses the challenges and potential solutions for scaling and cascading ONNs. Despite its technical depth and breadth, the paper is criticized for its lack of novelty and the need for more detailed comparisons and discussions on the practical implementation of photonic MVMs.
