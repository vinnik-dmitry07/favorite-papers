 **Summary:**
The paper introduces the concept of an "embodied Turing test" to benchmark AI systems against animal behaviors, suggesting that AI should emulate the sensorimotor capabilities of animals to achieve human-level intelligence. The authors argue that current AI systems lack the adaptability, flexibility, and energy efficiency of biological systems, and propose a research program to bridge the gap between AI and neuroscience. They suggest that understanding the neural circuits and biophysics of the brain could provide insights for AI, potentially leading to more efficient and adaptable AI systems. The paper also discusses the need for a shared platform and a new generation of researchers to drive this research forward.
