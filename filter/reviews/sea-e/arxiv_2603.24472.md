 **Summary:**
The paper investigates the impact of self-distillation on mathematical reasoning in large language models (LLMs), focusing on how it affects the expression of uncertainty and reasoning performance. The study reveals that self-distillation, while effective in some domains, can suppress epistemic verbalization, leading to a decrease in reasoning performance in mathematical reasoning tasks. This is attributed to the suppression of uncertainty expression, which is crucial for robust reasoning and optimization across diverse tasks. The paper provides a detailed analysis of how different types of self-distillation affect reasoning performance and offers insights into the role of epistemic verbalization in mathematical reasoning.
