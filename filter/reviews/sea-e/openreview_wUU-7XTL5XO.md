 **Summary:**
The paper introduces a benchmark for evaluating the reasoning capabilities of large language models (LLMs) in the context of planning and reasoning about actions and change. The benchmark, which includes a suite of test cases, is designed to assess the performance of LLMs in planning and replanning tasks, using a symbolic planning language to generate prompts for the models. The tests are evaluated against human performance, demonstrating that current LLMs, such as GPT-3 and BLOOM, struggle with these tasks. The paper also discusses the limitations of current benchmarks and proposes a framework for evaluating LLMs in more complex reasoning scenarios.
