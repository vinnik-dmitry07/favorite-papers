 **Summary:**
The paper presents a comprehensive review of the temporal derivative model (TDM) of neocortical learning, which integrates computational, algorithmic, and implementational aspects. The TDM proposes a novel mechanism for error-driven learning via temporal derivatives, facilitated by bidirectional connections and thalamocortical feedback. The model is supported by empirical evidence from neuroscience and is demonstrated to be a viable alternative to traditional backpropagation in neural networks. The paper discusses the TDM's ability to satisfy three criteria for a sufficient account of neocortical learning: computational, algorithmic, and implementational. It also critiques other learning models, such as Hebbian learning and predictive coding, and discusses their limitations.

- The paper critiques other learning models, such as Hebbian learning and predictive coding, and discusses their limitations, providing a robust evaluation of the TDM against existing models.
