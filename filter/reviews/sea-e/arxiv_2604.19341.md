 **Summary:**
The paper introduces SimpleTES, a novel framework for scaling AI-driven scientific discovery by structuring the evaluation process across multiple trajectories, iterative refinement, local candidate selection, and selective reuse of evaluated histories. This framework leverages a single open-source GPT-OSS model to achieve state-of-the-art solutions across 28 open-ended problems in diverse scientific domains, including quantum physics, astronomy, biology, AI, and mathematics. The framework's effectiveness is demonstrated through extensive experiments, showing significant improvements over existing methods. The paper also discusses the potential of post-training the model to enhance its performance further.

**Strengths:**
- The paper is well-written, clear, and easy to follow, with a clear motivation and a well-defined problem.
- The proposed framework, SimpleTES, is novel and innovative, providing a structured approach to scaling AI-driven scientific discovery.
- The framework is evaluated on a diverse set of problems across multiple domains, demonstrating its versatility and effectiveness.
- The paper provides a comprehensive evaluation of the framework, including a detailed analysis of the results and a post-training process that enhances the model's performance.
