 **Summary:**
The paper introduces a novel framework named CHORD, which integrates Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and Reinforcement Learning (RL) to address the challenges of disrupting established response patterns and overfitting in Large Language Models (LLMs). CHORD employs a dual-control mechanism, including a global coefficient and a token-wise weighting function, to manage the influence of off-policy expert data and ensure training stability. The framework is evaluated across various practical tasks, demonstrating significant improvements over existing methods. The paper provides a systematic analysis of the training dynamics when integrating off-policy expert knowledge into models with established policies, highlighting the "shift-readapt-overfit" progression. The proposed method, CHORD, offers a unified approach that harmonizes SFT and RL, providing a flexible control mechanism for managing the influence of off-policy expert data.

**Strengths:**
- The paper is well-written, clear, and easy to follow, with a clear motivation and a well-designed framework that effectively integrates Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and Reinforcement Learning (RL) to address the challenges of disrupting established response patterns and overfitting in Large Language Models (LLMs).
- The proposed method, CHORD, is novel and innovative, providing a unified approach that harmonizes SFT and RL, which is a significant contribution to the field.
- The paper includes extensive experiments that demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method, showing improvements over existing methods in various practical tasks.
