 **Summary:**
The paper investigates the phenomenon of "emergent abilities" in large language models (LLMs), challenging the notion that these abilities are intrinsic to the models. The authors argue that the apparent emergence of new capabilities in LLMs is largely an artifact of the metrics used to evaluate them, particularly those that are non-linear or discontinuous. The paper presents a mathematical model and empirical evidence to support this claim, including experiments on various tasks such as arithmetic and vision. It demonstrates that by changing the evaluation metric, the emergence of new abilities can be either enhanced or suppressed, suggesting that the observed emergent properties are more a function of the metrics used than the models themselves. The paper also discusses the implications of these findings for the design and interpretation of future LLM evaluations.

**Strengths:**
- The paper is well-written, clear, and easy to follow, with a clear and coherent message that is well-supported by the data.
- The authors provide a clear alternative explanation for the emergence of new capabilities in LLMs, which is a significant contribution to the field.
- The experiments are well-designed and convincingly demonstrate the role of metrics in shaping the observed emergent properties of LLMs.
- The paper provides a comprehensive analysis of the phenomenon, including a mathematical model and multiple experiments to support the claims.
- The findings have implications for the design of future benchmarks and evaluations of LLMs, which could help avoid misleading conclusions based on inappropriate metrics.

**Weaknesses:**
- The paper lacks a detailed discussion on the limitations of the proposed alternative explanation, particularly the assumptions made in the mathematical model.
- The paper could benefit from more extensive experiments to test the robustness of the findings across different model families and evaluation metrics.
- The paper does not sufficiently address the potential impact of the findings on the broader community, especially in terms of how it might affect the perception and evaluation of LLMs.
- The paper could be improved by including more detailed discussions on the implications of the findings for the design of future benchmarks and evaluations.
- The paper could benefit from a more thorough discussion on the potential societal implications of the findings, particularly in terms of the ethical considerations around the use of LLMs.

**Questions:**
- Could the authors provide a more detailed discussion on the limitations of the proposed alternative explanation, particularly the assumptions made in the mathematical model?
- How do the findings of this paper relate to the broader discussion on the limitations of current LLMs, and what implications might this have for the development of future models?
- Could the authors elaborate on the potential societal implications of the findings, particularly in terms of the ethical considerations around the use of LLMs?
- How might the findings of this paper influence the design of future benchmarks and evaluations of LLMs, and what steps might be taken to ensure that the metrics used do not inadvertently shape the observed capabilities of the models?
