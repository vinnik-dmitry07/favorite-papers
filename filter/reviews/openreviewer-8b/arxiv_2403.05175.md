## Strengths
The paper is well-written and provides a good overview of the field of continual learning. It is easy to follow and well-structured. The figures are helpful in illustrating the different concepts. The paper also provides a good overview of the different learning settings and evaluation metrics. The paper also discusses the different approaches that have been proposed in the literature and provides a good overview of the different techniques.

## Weaknesses
1. The paper does not provide a new method or a new analysis. It is simply an overview of the field. Therefore, it is not clear what the contribution of the paper is. Is it meant to be an overview for people who are new to the field? If so, it might be a good idea to add more details and explanations. For example, in section 2.4, it would be helpful to provide a few more sentences explaining the different settings and when they are used. In section 3.1, it would be helpful to provide a few more details about the different replay methods. 

2. The paper does not cover some of the latest developments in the field. For example, it does not discuss the use of prompting in continual learning (see [1, 2] for example). It also does not discuss the use of pre-trained models in continual learning (see [3, 4] for example).
