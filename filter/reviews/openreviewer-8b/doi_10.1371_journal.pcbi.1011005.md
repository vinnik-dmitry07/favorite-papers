# Review

## Summary
The authors propose a novel method to estimate causal effects of neurons on rewards in spiking neural networks. The method is based on the regression discontinuity design (RDD) approach to causal inference which is popular in economics. The authors show that the proposed method provides an unbiased estimate of the causal effect and can be used to optimize reward.

## Strengths
The paper is well written and the proposed method is novel and interesting. The method is well-motivated and the authors provide a thorough analysis of its properties and demonstrate its effectiveness in a few different settings.

## Weaknesses
The authors could have provided more details on how the proposed method can be implemented in practice. Additionally, it would have been nice to see some experimental results on real-world data.

## Questions
1. How do the proposed methods perform on real-world datasets?
2. How do the computational costs of the proposed method compare to other methods for estimating causal effects?
3. What are the limitations of the proposed method and when do they apply?
