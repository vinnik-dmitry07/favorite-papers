# Review

## Summary
This paper studies the performance of MLPs on vision tasks when trained with modern settings, including pretraining on large datasets, transfer learning, and test-time augmentations. The authors find that MLPs can achieve surprisingly strong performance when subjected to large scale training, and thus conclude that inductive bias can be compensated by scale.

## Strengths
- The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
- The experiments are comprehensive and convincing. The authors study MLPs in a variety of settings, including training from scratch, transfer learning, and test-time augmentations. The authors also study the scaling law of MLPs, which is a very interesting and important topic.
- The finding that larger batch sizes generalize better for MLPs is very interesting and not something that I have seen before in the literature.
