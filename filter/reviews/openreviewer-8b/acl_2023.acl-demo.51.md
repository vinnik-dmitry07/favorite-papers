# Review

## Summary
The paper presents a novel multilingual approach to one-hop KGQA, which compares favorably to strong baselines, such as T5 QA system, and previous embedding-based methods on a battery of benchmarks. The authors also release pre-trained models and provide an API making seamless integration into NLP applications possible.

## Strengths
1. The paper presents a novel multilingual approach to one-hop KGQA, which compares favorably to strong baselines, such as T5 QA system, and previous embedding-based methods on a battery of benchmarks.
2. The authors release pre-trained models and provide an API making seamless integration into NLP applications possible.

## Weaknesses
1. The paper only focuses on one-hop KGQA, and does not explore the generalization to multi-hop KGQA.
2. The paper does not provide a detailed analysis of the computational efficiency of the proposed method, especially when dealing with large-scale knowledge graphs.
3. The paper does not provide a detailed analysis of the interpretability of the proposed method, especially regarding how the different components of the model contribute to the final results.

## Questions
1. How does the proposed method generalize to multi-hop KGQA?
2. How is the computational efficiency of the proposed method, especially when dealing with large-scale knowledge graphs?
3. How is the interpretability of the proposed method, especially regarding how the different components of the model contribute to the final results?
