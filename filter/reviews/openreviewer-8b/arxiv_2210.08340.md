# Review

## Summary
This paper presents a perspective on the current state of AI research and its limitations, and argues for the need to incorporate more biological intelligence in AI systems. It also presents a proposal for an embodied Turing test to evaluate AI systems based on their ability to exhibit intelligent behavior in interaction with their environment, similar to living organisms.

## Strengths
The paper is well-written and presents a clear and compelling argument for the need to incorporate more biological intelligence in AI systems. The idea of an embodied Turing test is interesting and could be a useful framework for evaluating AI systems.

## Weaknesses
The paper is a perspective piece and does not present any new research results or proposals. It is also not clear how the embodied Turing test proposed in the paper can be operationalized or what the next steps would be to implement such a test. The paper could benefit from more concrete examples or case studies to illustrate the concepts discussed. Additionally, the paper could be strengthened by addressing the potential ethical implications of creating embodied Turing tests and how to ensure that they are conducted in a responsible and ethical manner.

## Questions
