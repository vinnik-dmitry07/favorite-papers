# Review

## Summary
This paper summarizes the recent progress of deep learning applications in stock market prediction. The authors present a general workflow for stock market prediction, categorizing different data sources, neural network structures, and evaluation metrics. They emphasize implementation and reproducibility, providing a valuable resource for researchers looking to enter this field. The paper also highlights several future research directions, helping readers stay current with the latest advancements.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-structured and easy to follow.
2. The paper provides a thorough review of the recent advancements in deep learning applications for stock market prediction, covering various data sources, neural network structures, and evaluation metrics.
3. The authors emphasize implementation and reproducibility, providing a valuable resource for researchers looking to enter this field.

## Weaknesses
1. The paper lacks a critical evaluation of the methods it presents. While it provides a comprehensive overview of the recent progress in deep learning applications for stock market prediction, it would be beneficial to include a critical analysis of the methods discussed. This analysis could involve evaluating the strengths and weaknesses of each method, discussing their practical implications, and providing insights into their potential impact on the field.
2. The paper does not provide a clear framework or guidelines for selecting the appropriate method for a given context. With so many methods to choose from, it can be overwhelming for researchers to decide which one to use. A more detailed comparison of the methods, including their advantages and disadvantages, would help researchers make informed decisions.
3. The paper could benefit from a more detailed discussion on the interpretability of the methods. While the authors mention the importance of interpretability in the introduction, the methods discussed in the paper may lack clarity in terms of how they make their predictions. Understanding the reasoning behind the methods' predictions is crucial for building trust and making informed decisions in the financial market.
