# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes a new benchmark for evaluating LLMs on reasoning about actions and change. The benchmark is based on the kinds of domains employed in the International Planning Competitions and evaluates 7 different aspects of reasoning about actions and change. The results on GPT-3 and BLOOM show subpar performance on such reasoning tasks.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
2. The paper provides a new benchmark for evaluating LLMs on reasoning about actions and change. The benchmark is based on the kinds of domains employed in the International Planning Competitions, which is more challenging than previous benchmarks.
3. The paper evaluates 7 different aspects of reasoning about actions and change, which is more comprehensive than previous benchmarks.
4. The paper provides detailed analysis of the performance of GPT-3 and BLOOM on the benchmark.

## Weaknesses
1. The paper only evaluates two LLMs, GPT-3 and BLOOM. It would be more convincing to evaluate more LLMs, such as PaLM, GLaM, Megatron-Turing NLG, Meta-OPT, Gopher, LaMDA, and Chinchilla.
2. The paper only provides the overall performance of LLMs on the benchmark. It would be more informative to provide the performance of LLMs on different tasks in the benchmark.

## Questions
1. Why only evaluate GPT-3 and BLOOM? Why not evaluate other LLMs such as PaLM, GLaM, Megatron-Turing NLG, Meta-OPT, Gopher, LaMDA, and Chinchilla?
2. What is the performance of LLMs on different tasks in the benchmark?
