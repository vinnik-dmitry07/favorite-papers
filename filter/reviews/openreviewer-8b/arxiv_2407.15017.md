# Review

## Summary
This paper presents a survey of knowledge mechanisms in LLMs, encompassing knowledge acquisition, storage, utilization, and evolution. The authors introduce a novel taxonomy to structure the understanding of knowledge mechanisms in LLMs, focusing on knowledge utilization and evolution. They explore the ways LLMs acquire, store, utilize, and evolve knowledge, addressing the fragility of parametric knowledge and the concept of "dark knowledge" that LLMs have not yet learned. The paper aims to enhance the interpretability and reliability of LLMs by examining the mechanisms behind their knowledge processes.

## Strengths
1. The taxonomy of knowledge mechanisms in LLMs is a novel contribution, providing a comprehensive framework to understand the entire knowledge lifecycle.
2. The paper addresses a critical issue in AI by examining the fragility of knowledge in LLMs and the concept of "dark knowledge," which are crucial for improving the reliability and safety of LLMs.

## Weaknesses
1. The paper is densely packed with information and may be challenging for readers unfamiliar with the topic.
2. While the taxonomy is comprehensive, it might be overly complex for some readers to grasp the key points easily.

## Questions
1. How do the authors plan to make the content more accessible to readers who may not be familiar with the technical nuances of LLMs?
2. Are there any practical applications or experiments that the authors envision for validating the proposed taxonomy?
