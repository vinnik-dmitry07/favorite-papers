# Review

## Summary
This paper presents a study on the capabilities of GPT-4 in medical question answering, focusing on the potential of prompt engineering to enhance its performance without the need for intensive domain-specific training. The authors introduce a method called Medprompt, which combines various prompting strategies, including dynamic few-shot learning, self-generated chain of thought, and choice shuffling ensemble. The study demonstrates that Medprompt significantly improves GPT-4's performance on multiple medical QA datasets, surpassing state-of-the-art specialist models like Med-PaLM 2. The paper also highlights the generalizability of the approach by showing its effectiveness in other domains such as electrical engineering, machine learning, and philosophy.
