# Review

## Summary
This paper provides a comprehensive overview of the recent developments in self-supervised learning (SSL) methods for computer vision. The paper organizes the SSL methods into four main families: deep metric learning, self-distillation, canonical correlation analysis, and masked image modeling. The paper also discusses the role of data augmentation, the use of projectors, and the impact of pre-training data in SSL. The paper also provides some theoretical results that unify various SSL methods. Overall, the paper provides a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners in the field of SSL.
