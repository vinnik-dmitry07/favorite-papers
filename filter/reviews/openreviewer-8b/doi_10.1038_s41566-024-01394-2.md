# Review

## Summary
This paper provides a comprehensive overview of the recent developments and applications of photonic matrix multiplication (PMM). PMM uses light as a computing medium to perform matrix-vector multiplication, which is a fundamental operation in modern signal processing and artificial intelligence algorithms. The authors discuss three methods for PMM: the plane light conversion method, Mach-Zehnder interferometer method, and wavelength division multiplexing method. They summarize the key milestones in the development of PMM and its applications in optical signal processing and artificial neural networks. The paper also highlights the potential of PMM in expanding the domain of telecommunications and artificial intelligence due to its superior performance.

## Strengths
The paper provides a thorough review of the existing literature on photonic matrix multiplication, covering various methods and their applications. This comprehensive overview helps readers understand the current state-of-the-art in this field.

The authors discuss the potential of photonic matrix multiplication in expanding the domain of telecommunications and artificial intelligence. They highlight the superior performance of PMM in terms of speed and low latency, which is crucial for many real-time applications.

The paper summarizes key milestones in the development of photonic matrix multiplication and its applications. This historical context helps readers understand the progression of this technology.

## Weaknesses
While the paper provides a comprehensive overview of photonic matrix multiplication, it lacks in-depth analysis and comparison of the different methods. A more detailed discussion on the advantages and limitations of each method would have been beneficial.

The paper could have included more experimental results to support the claims made. Without experimental validation, the effectiveness and performance of the proposed methods may be questionable.

## Questions
