# Review

## Summary
This paper presents a comprehensive review of the recent progress in developing LLM agents, with a particular focus on the role of externalization. The authors argue that the capabilities of LLM agents are no longer primarily improved by increasing model size or complexity, but rather by designing and developing external structures that can handle various aspects of the task, such as memory, skills, and protocols. The paper provides a detailed overview of each of these areas, tracing their development from simple initial approaches to more sophisticated, modern systems. The authors also discuss the interactions between these different externalization methods and the need for a unifying "harness" that can coordinate and integrate them. The paper concludes by identifying some of the key challenges and potential directions for future research in this area.

## Strengths
1. The paper provides a novel perspective on the development of LLM agents by framing the progress in terms of externalization. This shifts the focus from purely increasing model capabilities to designing better external structures, which can be more reliable and efficient.
2. The paper is well-structured and clearly written, with each section building on the previous one. The use of figures and tables helps to illustrate key points and comparisons.
3. The paper offers a comprehensive review of recent developments in LLM agents, covering a wide range of relevant topics and approaches.

## Weaknesses
1. While the paper provides a detailed overview of existing methods, it could benefit from a more in-depth analysis of the trade-offs and limitations of different approaches. For example, what are the potential downsides of relying heavily on external memory? How do different skill externalization methods impact the overall performance and flexibility of the agent?
2. The paper could provide more concrete examples or case studies to illustrate the concepts and how they are applied in practice. This would help readers better understand the real-world implications of the proposed methods.
3. The paper could benefit from a more detailed discussion of the ethical and societal implications of the development of LLM agents, particularly in relation to the externalization of decision-making and the potential impacts on accountability and responsibility.

## Questions
1. How do the authors envision the future development of LLM agents evolving? What new challenges do you anticipate arising, and how might they be addressed?
2. Can you provide more specific examples of how externalization has impacted the performance of real-world LLM agents in practical applications?
3. What are the key factors determining the optimal balance between parametric capability and externalized infrastructure in LLM agents?
