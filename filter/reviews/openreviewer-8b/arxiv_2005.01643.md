# Review

## Summary
This paper presents a tutorial on offline reinforcement learning. It first introduces the problem setting and the basic concepts of RL. Then, it discusses some challenges of offline RL, and introduces some methods to address them. The paper also presents a few examples of scenarios where offline RL can be applied, and it concludes by identifying open problems in the field.

## Strengths
1. The paper provides a comprehensive overview of offline RL, including its formulation, challenges, and some existing methods to address them.
2. The paper discusses some fundamental issues of offline RL, such as distributional shift and the need for counterfactual queries.

## Weaknesses
1. The paper is not very well structured. It is not clear what is the main goal of the paper, and how the different sections are related. 
2. The paper does not provide a clear and concise summary of the current state of offline RL, and it does not clearly identify the most important open problems in the field. 
3. The paper does not provide any new theoretical or experimental results. It would be more useful to provide a new algorithm or a new theoretical analysis of existing methods.

## Questions
1. What is the main goal of the paper?
2. What are the most important open problems in offline RL?
