# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes a new model merging method called M2N2, which is based on an evolutionary algorithm. The algorithm has three key features: (1) dynamic adjustment of merging boundaries to progressively explore a broader range of parameter combinations; (2) a diversity preservation mechanism inspired by the competition for resources in nature, to maintain a population of diverse, high-performing models that are particularly well-suited for merging; and (3) a heuristic-based attraction metric to identify the most promising pairs of models for fusion. The experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method can be used to evolve models entirely from scratch, and can be used to merge specialized language and image generation models.
