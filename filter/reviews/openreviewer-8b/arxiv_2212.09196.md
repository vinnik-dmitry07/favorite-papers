# Review

## Summary
This paper presents an evaluation of GPT-3’s ability to perform analogical reasoning. The authors designed four different tasks to test GPT-3’s analogical abilities: 1) text-based matrix reasoning problems, 2) letter-string analogies, 3) four-term verbal analogies, and 4) story analogies. The authors compared GPT-3’s performance on these tasks with human performance and found that GPT-3 performed as well or better than humans in most cases.

## Strengths
- The paper is well-written and easy to follow. The authors clearly explain the motivation behind their work and the rationale for designing the four different tasks. The tasks themselves are well-designed and appropriate for evaluating analogical reasoning.
- The authors conducted extensive experiments with human participants and compared their performance with GPT-3. This provides a valuable benchmark for evaluating GPT-3’s abilities.
- The results are interesting and show that GPT-3 has acquired an emergent ability to reason by analogy, matching or surpassing human performance across a wide range of text-based problem types.

## Weaknesses
- The paper only evaluates GPT-3 and does not compare its performance with other language models. It would be interesting to see how other models, such as GPT-4 or PaLM, perform on these tasks.
- The paper does not provide a detailed analysis of GPT-3’s error patterns. It would be valuable to understand what types of analogies GPT-3 struggles with and how its error patterns differ from humans.
- The paper does not provide a detailed analysis of the impact of different prompting strategies on GPT-3’s performance. It would be interesting to see how different prompts affect its ability to reason by analogy.

## Questions
- Have you tried using different prompting strategies to see if they improve GPT-3’s performance on analogical reasoning tasks?
- Have you tried using GPT-3 to generate explanations for its reasoning process after it has generated the answer? This might provide insights into its strategy for solving analogical problems.
