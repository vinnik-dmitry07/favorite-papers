The paper presents a comprehensive review of the literature on catastrophic forgetting in deep learning, with a focus on methods that use gradient descent as their learning algorithm. The authors provide a taxonomy of existing techniques for mitigating catastrophic forgetting, and identify areas where further research is needed.

## Strengths
The paper provides a thorough review of the literature on catastrophic forgetting in deep learning, with a comprehensive taxonomy of existing techniques for mitigating this issue. The authors also identify several open problems and research gaps in this area.

## Weaknesses
1. The paper is more of a literature review than a research paper. It does not propose any new techniques or methods to address the problem of catastrophic forgetting.
2. The paper is missing some key references on catastrophic forgetting in deep learning, such as:
- "Overcoming catastrophic forgetting in neural networks by Kirkpatrick et al., 2017"
- "Gradient Episodic Memory for Continual Learning by Lopez-Paz et al., 2017"
- "Continual learning through synaptic intelligence by Madireddy et al., 2020"
3. The paper is not well-written and is full of grammatical errors and typos. It is also not well-structured, making it difficult to follow the flow of the paper.
