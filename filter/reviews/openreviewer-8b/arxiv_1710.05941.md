# Review

## Summary
This paper presents a new activation function Swish, which is discovered by using a combination of exhaustive and reinforcement learning-based search. The authors verify the effectiveness of the activation function by conducting an empirical evaluation with the best discovered activation function on several datasets.

## Strengths
1. The proposed method is simple and easy to follow.
2. The proposed method achieves good performance on several datasets.

## Weaknesses
1. The novelty of the proposed method is limited. The Swish function is a simple modification of the Sigmoid function. 
2. The authors do not provide a theoretical analysis of the Swish function.
3. The authors do not provide the code in the supplementary material.

## Questions
1. The Swish function is a simple modification of the Sigmoid function. What is the novelty of the proposed method?
2. Can the authors provide a theoretical analysis of the Swish function?
3. Can the authors provide the code in the supplementary material for reviewers' inspection?
