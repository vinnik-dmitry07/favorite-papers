# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes a non-parametric approach that evolves via reinforcement learning on episodic memory. Specifically, the authors propose to filter noise and identify high-utility strategies through environmental feedback. The authors conduct extensive experiments on HLE, BigCodeBench, ALFWorld, and Lifelong Agent Bench to show the effectiveness of the proposed method.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
2. The idea of using RL on memory is interesting.
3. The experiments are extensive and the results are promising.

## Weaknesses
1. The idea of using RL on memory is interesting. However, the proposed method seems to be straightforward and lacks novelty. The authors should further highlight the novelty of the proposed method.
2. The authors should provide more details about the baselines. It is unclear why the proposed method can outperform the baselines by a large margin. 
3. The authors should provide the standard deviation of the results in Table 1 and Table 2.
4. The authors should provide the computation cost of the proposed method.
