# Review

## Summary
The authors show that transformers with recurrent positional encodings can learn spatial representations similar to those found in the rodent hippocampus and entorhinal cortex. They also show that the transformer is closely related to the Tolman-Eichenbaum Machine (TEM), a model of the hippocampus. Finally, they discuss how their results might generalize to other cognitive domains, such as language.

## Weaknesses

- The authors do not provide any suggestions for future research.

## Questions
