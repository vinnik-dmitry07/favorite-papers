## Summary
This paper presents a method called FunSearch, which combines LLMs with an evolutionary algorithm to discover new solutions for mathematical and algorithmic problems. The method is applied to the cap set problem in extremal combinatorics and the online bin packing problem in combinatorial optimization, achieving state-of-the-art results in both areas. The key innovation of FunSearch is its ability to search for programs generating solutions, rather than the solutions themselves, making the discovered solutions more interpretable and concise. The paper demonstrates the effectiveness of FunSearch in producing novel and impactful results in mathematical sciences.
