# Review

## Summary
The paper presents a general architecture for autonomous intelligence that is composed of a number of modules that are differentiable and many of which are trainable. The architecture includes a configurable world model that can learn a hierarchy of representations, and a non-contrastive self-supervised learning paradigm that produces representations that are both informative and predictable. The paper also describes how to use the world model for hierarchical planning under uncertainty. The paper is a position paper that expresses a vision for a path towards intelligent machines that learn more like animals and humans, that can reason and plan, and whose behavior is driven by intrinsic objectives, rather than by hard-wired programs, external supervision, or external rewards.
