# Why mathematics is set to be revolutionized by AI

## Summary
This article discusses the potential impact of AI on the field of mathematics, particularly in generating conjectures. The authors argue that while the role of machines in formulating conjectures may seem counterintuitive, it is an area where machine intelligence can significantly contribute to mathematical progress. They suggest that AI can aid in the discovery of new conjectures, which can then be verified and expanded upon by human mathematicians. The article emphasizes the potential for AI to help advance mathematical research and make significant contributions to the field.

## Strengths
The article explores the innovative use of AI in mathematics, specifically its potential to generate conjectures that can drive new mathematical discoveries. It challenges the traditional view that conjecture formulation is solely a human endeavor, opening up new avenues for AI-assisted mathematical research.

## Weaknesses
1. The article could benefit from a more detailed explanation of how AI can generate conjectures independently and the underlying mechanisms it uses. While the authors mention intuition and experience, a deeper dive into the methods would enhance the reader's understanding.
2. The article primarily focuses on the potential of AI without discussing the existing challenges or limitations. A balanced view that includes the current hurdles in integrating AI into mathematical conjecture generation would provide a more comprehensive perspective.
3. The article could include examples or case studies where AI has already contributed to or failed to contribute to conjecture generation. This would help readers better understand the practical implications and the potential pitfalls of AI in this context.

## Questions
1. Can the authors provide more details on the mechanisms AI uses to generate conjectures? How does it combine intuition and experience?
2. What are the current challenges or limitations of AI in this field? How can these be addressed?
3. Can the authors provide specific examples of AI-generated conjectures and their outcomes, including failures?
