# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes a new pretraining objective for multi-document summarization. The authors propose a new masking strategy for the Gap Sentence Generation (GSG) objective, which is to mask sentences that are most likely to be included in the summary. The authors use the frequency of entities to identify such sentences. The proposed method is tested in zero-shot, few-shot and full fine-tuning settings and shows better performance than other pre-trained models.

## Strengths
1. The proposed method is simple and easy to understand.
2. The proposed method shows better performance than other pre-trained models in zero-shot and few-shot settings.

## Weaknesses
1. The proposed method is not tested in the full fine-tuning setting. The authors claim that the proposed method is designed to reduce the need for large amounts of fine-tuning labeled data. However, the authors do not show that the proposed method can outperform other models trained with the full fine-tuning setting.
2. The proposed method is not compared with other multi-document summarization methods. The authors only compare the proposed method with other pre-trained models. However, there are many neural multi-document summarization methods that are not pre-trained (e.g. [1]). The authors should compare the proposed method with these methods.
3. The proposed method is not evaluated with other metrics. The authors only use ROUGE to evaluate the generated summaries. However, ROUGE has been shown to be an inaccurate metric for summarization (e.g. [2]). The authors should use other metrics, such as BertScore [3].

[1] Multi-News: A Large-scale Multi-document Summarization Dataset and Abstractive Hierarchical Model

[2] SummaRuNN: A Neural Abstractive Document Summarization Approach Based on Hierarchical Neural Retrieval

[3] BERTScore: Evaluating Text Summarization with BERT

## Questions
1. Why is the proposed method not tested in the full fine-tuning setting?
2. Why are other multi-document summarization methods not compared with the proposed method?
3. Why are other metrics not used to evaluate the generated summaries?
