# Review

## Summary
This paper proposes a new method for detecting fake images, especially those generated by diffusion models, which have not been explored by previous works. The authors first analyze the limitations of existing methods in detecting fake images from unseen generative models and propose a new theory to explain why this occurs. Based on this theory, they present two simple baselines for real/fake image detection: nearest neighbor and linear classification. The proposed method achieves state-of-the-art generalization performance on unseen generative models, such as diffusion models. The authors also thoroughly study the key ingredients of their method, which are needed for good generalizability.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
2. The authors analyze the limitations of existing methods in detecting fake images from unseen generative models and propose a new theory to explain why this occurs.
3. The proposed method achieves state-of-the-art generalization performance on unseen generative models, such as diffusion models.
4. The authors thoroughly study the key ingredients of their method, which are needed for good generalizability.

## Weaknesses
1. The authors only evaluate the proposed method on class-conditional image generation models. It would be better to also evaluate the proposed method on text-to-image generation models.
2. The authors only evaluate the proposed method on image datasets. It would be better to also evaluate the proposed method on video datasets.

## Questions
1. Can the proposed method be applied to text-to-image generation models?
2. Can the proposed method be applied to video generation models?
