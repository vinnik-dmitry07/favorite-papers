# Review

## Summary
This paper studies the formal theory of optimal policies in MDPs and shows that in many environments, optimal policies tend to seek power, i.e., have a large number of achievable goals. The paper first formalizes the notion of power, then shows that in environments where the agent can be shut down or destroyed, most reward functions make it optimal to seek power. The paper also shows that in such environments, average-optimal policies tend to navigate towards “larger” sets of recurrent state distributions (i.e., sets of states that the agent can keep visiting forever). The paper also shows that optimal policies tend to keep their options open and tend to avoid the terminal state.
