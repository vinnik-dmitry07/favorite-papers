# Review

## Summary
This paper introduces Llama 3, a new set of foundation models that excel in multilingual, coding, reasoning, and tool usage. The largest model in the herd is a dense Transformer with 405B parameters and a context window of up to 128K tokens. Extensive empirical evaluations show that Llama 3 delivers comparable quality to leading language models such as GPT-4 across various tasks. The paper also details the development of multimodal extensions for image, video, and speech recognition, which perform competitively with state-of-the-art models in their respective fields. The authors publicly release Llama 3 models, including pre-trained and post-trained versions of the 405B parameter model and Llama Guard 3 for safety, to advance AI research and development.
