# Review

## Summary
This paper introduces Super-Natural Instructions, a benchmark of 1,616 diverse NLP tasks and their expert-written instructions. The collection covers 76 distinct task types, including classification, extraction, in- 178 filling, sequence tagging, text rewriting, and text composition. This large and diverse collection of tasks enables rigorous benchmarking of cross-task generalization under instructions— training models to follow instructions on a subset of tasks and evaluating them on the remaining unseen ones. Furthermore, the authors build Tk-Instruct, a transformer model trained to follow a variety of in-context instructions (plain language task definitions or k-shot examples). The experiments show that Tk-Instruct outperforms existing instruction-following models such as InstructGPT by over 9% on our benchmark despite being an order of magnitude smaller. The authors also analyze generalization as a function of various scaling parameters, such as the number of observed tasks, the number of instances per task, and model sizes. The authors hope their dataset and model facilitate future progress towards more general-purpose NLP models.

## Strengths
- The paper introduces Super-Natural Instructions, a benchmark of 1,616 diverse NLP tasks and their expert-written instructions, which enables rigorous benchmarking of cross-task generalization under instructions.
- The paper also presents Tk-Instruct, a transformer model trained to follow a variety of in-context instructions, which outperforms existing instruction-following models such as InstructGPT by over 9% on the Super-Natural Instructions benchmark despite being an order of magnitude smaller.
- The paper provides an analysis of generalization as a function of various scaling parameters, such as the number of observed tasks, the number of instances per task, and model sizes, which provides insights into the factors that affect the generalization of NLP models.

## Weaknesses
- The paper does not provide a detailed comparison of the Super-Natural Instructions benchmark with other existing benchmarks, such as the NL2Bash benchmark, which also includes a diverse set of NLP tasks and their instructions.
- The paper does not provide a detailed analysis of the quality of the expert-written instructions in the Super-Natural Instructions benchmark, such as the clarity, sufficiency, and consistency of the instructions.
- The paper does not provide a detailed analysis of the diversity of the NLP tasks in the Super-Natural Instructions benchmark, such as the distribution of task types, domains, and languages, and how they compare to other benchmarks.

## Questions
- How does the Super-Natural Instructions benchmark compare to other existing benchmarks, such as the NL2Bash benchmark, in terms of the diversity and coverage of NLP tasks and their instructions?
- What is the quality of the expert-written instructions in the Super-Natural Instructions benchmark, and how is it ensured that the instructions are clear, sufficient, and consistent?
- What is the diversity of the NLP tasks in the Super-Natural Instructions benchmark, and how does it compare to other benchmarks in terms of the distribution of task types, domains, and languages?
- How does the performance of Tk-Instruct compare to other state-of-the-art models on the Super-Natural Instructions benchmark, and what are the factors that contribute to its superior performance?
- What are the limitations of the Super-Natural Instructions benchmark and how can it be improved in the future to better capture the diversity and complexity of NLP tasks?
