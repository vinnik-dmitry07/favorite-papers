### Strengths

### Weaknesses

I have two questions about the paper.

1. The authors use the ETHICS dataset to evaluate the metacognitive abilities of LLMs. However, I wonder whether this dataset is suitable for this purpose. The dataset contains sentences with moral labels, and the authors use logistic regression to identify the target axes. I wonder whether the target axes are related to the moral labels. If they are not, then it is not clear whether the metacognitive abilities of LLMs are related to the moral labels.

2. The authors show that LLMs can only monitor a small subset of their neural activations. I wonder whether this is because the LLMs are not powerful enough. The authors use LLaMA 3.1 8B and 70B, which are relatively small models. I wonder whether larger models can monitor more neural activations.
