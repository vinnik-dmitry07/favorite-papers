### Summary

The paper argues that the representations in AI models are converging. The authors show that different neural networks represent data in a more aligned way over time and across multiple domains. They also show that vision models and language models are converging across data modalities. The authors hypothesize that this convergence is driving toward a shared statistical model of reality, akin to Plato’s concept of an ideal reality.
