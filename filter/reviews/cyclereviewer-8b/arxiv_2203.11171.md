### Summary

This paper proposes a new decoding strategy for chain-of-thought prompting, called self-consistency. The method involves sampling a diverse set of reasoning paths and then selecting the most consistent answer by marginalizing out the sampled reasoning paths. The authors evaluate self-consistency on a range of arithmetic and commonsense reasoning benchmarks and show that it improves over chain-of-thought prompting by a significant margin.
