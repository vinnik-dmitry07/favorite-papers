### Summary

This paper investigates how LLMs store and extract knowledge from pretraining data. The authors argue that LLMs can memorize knowledge from the pretraining data but struggle to extract it during inference. To address this issue, they propose a controlled biography dataset and employ probing techniques to assess how knowledge augmentation influences the extractability of knowledge in pre-trained transformer models. The authors also suggest that rewriting critical but infrequent data during the pretrain stage can enhance knowledge extraction for downstream tasks.
