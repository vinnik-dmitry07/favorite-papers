### Summary

This paper investigates the effect of RL on LLMs and proposes a RL-free method for reasoning. The authors claim that RL does not teach new strategies; it redistributes probability mass over solutions the base model already contains. The authors then propose a new method that matches the performance of RL but with much less compute and data.
