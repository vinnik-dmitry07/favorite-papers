## Reviewer

### Summary

This paper presents a study on the impact of model size on the generalization of dual encoders for dense retrieval. The authors propose a model called Generalizable T5 Retriever (GTR) that uses the T5 model architecture and scales up its size while keeping the bottleneck embedding size fixed. The authors conduct experiments on the BEIR benchmark and show that the GTR model outperforms existing sparse and dense retrievers. The paper also presents an ablation study that finds that the GTR model is highly data efficient, requiring only 10% of the MS Marco supervised data to achieve the best out-of-domain performance.
