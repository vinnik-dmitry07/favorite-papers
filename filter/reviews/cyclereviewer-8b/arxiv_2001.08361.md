## Summary

This paper studies the scaling laws for language model performance on the cross-entropy loss. The authors show that the loss scales as a power-law with model size, dataset size, and the amount of compute used for training. The authors also show that overfitting depends predictably on model/dataset size and the dependence of training speed on model size. The authors also show that larger models are significantly more sample-efficient and that optimal training involves training very large models on a relatively modest amount of data and stopping significantly before convergence.
