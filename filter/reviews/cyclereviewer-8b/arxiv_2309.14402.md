### Summary

The paper investigates the ability of language models to perform four basic knowledge manipulation tasks: retrieval, classification, comparison, and inverse search. The authors find that language models excel in knowledge retrieval but struggle with the other tasks, especially when they are not trained and prompted in a Chain of Thoughts (CoT) manner. They also find that language models cannot perform inverse knowledge search, regardless of the prompts. The authors suggest that these limitations are inherent to language models and propose several strategies to improve their knowledge manipulation abilities, including generating more CoT data and employing methods like retrieval augmented generation and reversal training.
