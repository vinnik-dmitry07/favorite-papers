### Summary

The paper presents a novel approach to self-play in adaptive synthetic environments. The authors introduce SPADE, a framework that leverages a single Large Language Model (LLM) to play two roles: an Environment Designer and a Reasoning Agent. The Environment Designer generates complete, long-horizon training environments as executable code, while the Reasoning Agent learns to act within these environments. The authors demonstrate that SPADE outperforms fixed-environment baselines on various benchmarks, showcasing its potential for open-ended self-improvement.
