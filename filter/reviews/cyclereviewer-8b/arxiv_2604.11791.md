### Summary

This paper studies the behavior of looped LLMs, which are LLMs that allow for multiple applications of the same layer stack to the hidden states. The authors study the behavior of the looped LLMs in terms of the stages of inference, which are the different behaviors that different layers exhibit as you go through the layers of a transformer. The authors show that looped LLMs tend to have similar stages of inference as feedforward LLMs, and that the stages of inference in looped LLMs are stable across different numbers of iterations of the loop. The authors also study the impact of different architectural choices on the behavior of looped LLMs.
