### Summary

This paper proposes LeJEPA, a new self-supervised learning method that is theoretically grounded and scalable. The authors first prove that the optimal distribution for the embeddings is isotropic Gaussian. Then, they introduce a new objective, Sketched Isotropic Gaussian Regularization (SIGReg), to enforce the distribution of the embeddings to be isotropic Gaussian. LeJEPA combines the JEPA predictive loss with SIGReg. The authors show that LeJEPA has several benefits: (i) single trade-off hyperparameter, (ii) linear time and memory complexity, (iii) stability across hyperparameters, architectures, and domains, (iv) heuristics-free, and (v) distributed training-friendly implementation. The authors validate LeJEPA on 10+ datasets, 60+ architectures, and show that it outperforms state-of-the-art methods.
