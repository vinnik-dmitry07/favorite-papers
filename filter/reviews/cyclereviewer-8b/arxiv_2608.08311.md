### Summary

The paper presents a self-developing agent called Ouroboros, which continuously improves its own tools, context, prompts, and core implementation through reviewed commits. The agent operates in two modes: recursive free evolution and experience-driven core evolution. The paper also introduces Hope, a 161-day experiment with Ouroboros, where the agent interacts with humans and continuously evolves its implementation. The paper also discusses the operational safety architecture of Ouroboros, which ensures that the agent's evolution does not compromise its operational safety.
