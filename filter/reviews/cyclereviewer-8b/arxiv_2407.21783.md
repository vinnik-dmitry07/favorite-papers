### Summary

The paper introduces Llama 3, a new set of foundation models designed to support multilingual capabilities, coding, reasoning, and tool usage. The authors highlight three key factors in the development of high-quality foundation models: data, scale, and managing complexity. They present a new set of foundation models, Llama 3, which is a herd of language models that natively support multilinguality, coding, reasoning, and tool usage. The largest model is a dense Transformer with 405B parameters and a context window of up to 128K tokens. The paper provides an extensive empirical evaluation of Llama 3 and finds that it delivers comparable quality to leading language models such as GPT-4 on a variety of tasks. The paper also presents results of experiments in which image, video, and speech capabilities are integrated into Llama 3 via a compositional approach, and observe that this approach performs competitively with the state-of-the-art on image, video, and speech recognition tasks. The resulting models are not yet being broadly released as they are still under development.
