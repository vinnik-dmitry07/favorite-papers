### Summary

The paper presents MLE-bench, a benchmark designed to evaluate AI agents' capabilities in machine learning engineering tasks. The benchmark consists of 75 Kaggle competitions, which are considered challenging and representative of contemporary ML engineering work. The authors use open-source agent scaffolds to evaluate several frontier language models on the benchmark, finding that the best-performing setup achieves at least the level of a Kaggle bronze medal in 16.9% of competitions. The authors also investigate various forms of resource-scaling for AI agents and the impact of contamination from pre-training.
