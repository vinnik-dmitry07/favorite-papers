## Summary

This paper shows that supervised fine-tuning (SFT) on curated data can be understood as a lower bound on the RL objective in the sparse reward setting. Then the authors propose a new variant of SFT, called importance weighted supervised fine-tuning (iw-SFT), which optimizes a tighter bound to the RL objective. The authors show that this new variant can improve performance compared to SFT on curated data and can be further generalized to training with quality scored data. The authors also show that the resulting SFT variants are competitive with more advanced RL algorithms for large language models and for training policies in continuous control tasks.
