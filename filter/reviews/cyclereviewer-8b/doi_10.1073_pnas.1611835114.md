## Summary

This paper proposes a method to overcome catastrophic forgetting in neural networks. The method is based on the idea that the network should learn new tasks in a way that the parameters that are important for the previous tasks are not changed too much. This is achieved by adding a penalty term to the loss function that constrains the parameters to stay close to their old values. The authors show that their method can be used in supervised learning and reinforcement learning problems to train several tasks sequentially without forgetting older ones.
