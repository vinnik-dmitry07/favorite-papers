This paper studies the reasoning capabilities of LLMs on grade-school math problems. The authors construct a synthetic dataset, iGSM, which consists of math problems with a dependency graph and a solution chain. The authors then pre-train a GPT-2 model on the iGSM dataset and evaluate its performance on the same dataset. The authors also propose a probing task to understand the internal reasoning process of the model.
