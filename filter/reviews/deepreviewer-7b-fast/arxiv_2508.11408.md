## Summary:

This paper introduces Chord, a novel framework designed to harmonize supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and reinforcement learning (RL) for large language models (LLMs). The core motivation stems from the observation that while SFT can efficiently transfer established response patterns, it often struggles with generalization and can lead to overfitting. Conversely, RL encourages exploration but is prone to disrupting established patterns. Chord addresses this by reframing SFT as a dynamic, on-policy learning process, integrating expert data into the RL objective through a combination of a global coefficient and a token-wise weighting function. The global coefficient, μ, modulates the influence of expert data, while the token-wise function, ϕ, downweights tokens at both ends of the probability spectrum, preventing entropy collapse and encouraging exploration.
