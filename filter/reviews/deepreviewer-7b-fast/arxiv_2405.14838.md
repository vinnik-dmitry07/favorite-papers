This paper introduces Stepwise Internalization, a novel method for achieving implicit chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning in language models. The core idea is to progressively remove intermediate reasoning steps from explicit CoT prompts during fine-tuning, forcing the model to internalize the reasoning process. This is achieved by gradually reducing the number of CoT tokens during training, effectively transitioning the model from a state where it relies on explicit intermediate steps to one where it performs implicit reasoning. The authors demonstrate the efficacy of this approach across several reasoning tasks, including multi-digit multiplication and the grade-school math reasoning task, GSM8K.
