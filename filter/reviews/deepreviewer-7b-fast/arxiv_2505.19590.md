## Summary:

The authors propose Intuitor, an instance of RLIF that employs a modified Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) algorithm, replacing the traditional reward signal with a model's self-certainty score. This score, calculated as the average KL divergence between the model's output distribution and a uniform distribution, serves as an intrinsic reward, guiding the model's improvement through its own assessment of its outputs. The core idea is that a model can learn to improve without relying on external human-provided rewards or labeled data, potentially leading to more scalable and autonomous learning systems.
