This paper introduces On-Policy Self-Distillation (OPSD), a novel approach for enhancing the reasoning capabilities of large language models (LLMs) by leveraging the model's own capabilities for self-distillation. The core idea is to use a single LLM as both the teacher and the student, with the teacher providing dense, token-level supervision through a process of rationalization, while the student learns to generate rationales that align with the teacher's. The method operates on-policy, meaning the student is trained on its own generated trajectories, and employs a full-vocabulary logit distillation objective to match the teacher's and student's next-token distributions at each position.
