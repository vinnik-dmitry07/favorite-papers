This constraint hinders AD's ability to generalize to new action spaces without retraining. Headless-AD addresses this issue by removing the final linear layer of the transformer architecture, which previously mapped action embeddings to specific actions. Instead, Headless-AD directly predicts action *embeddings* within the transformer's output layer, allowing it to operate without a predefined action space.
