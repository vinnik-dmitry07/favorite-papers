The core idea revolves around the observation that, when regenerating the continuation of a CoT trace, correct answers tend to be more reproducible from the initial prefix than incorrect ones. This asymmetry forms the basis for a novel weighting scheme used within a weighted majority voting (WMV) framework. Specifically, the authors propose that if an answer is consistently reproduced through regeneration, it is more likely to be the correct answer.
